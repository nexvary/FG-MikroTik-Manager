FG MTM by FG Machines

A Windows workspace for MikroTik network management and synchronized business data.
Qt 6 / QML interface, C++20 engine. Arabic RTL and English.

This edition supports FG Server login, scoped business browsing and CSV export,
RADIUS users/sessions, server diagnostics, RouterOS 7 REST reads and a floating read terminal.
Passwords and login sessions are not saved. Trusted HTTPS is required.

Android parity is still in progress: voucher creation/printing, financial edits,
HotSpot setup, portal deployment, router writes and RouterOS 6 API are not included yet.

Use a separate Windows account in the same tenant and branch to avoid revoking
sessions on your phone or web panel. This installer does not modify your routers.
